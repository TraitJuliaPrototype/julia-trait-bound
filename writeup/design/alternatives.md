
- **Assignment into storage typed by the trait unwraps a `Dynamic{X}`** (written into the reference on 2026-11-11). Not possible: a `Dynamic{X}` is a member, and Julia stores a member without calling `convert`. A method `convert(::Type{T}, ::Dynamic{<:T})` is never reached there, and its definition logs 7,005 invalidation entries (`experiments/unification/w149/convert_probe.jl`). Replaced by the rule that `keep` is not used into such storage.
- **`Kept{X}` as a member of `X`** whose attribute a plain call reads. Not taken: the attribute of a plain call is a function of the method and the argument types, so a value's bit cannot decide it; a member would also be stored as it is in storage typed by the trait. `Kept{X}` is storage, read by `k[]`, `unwrap` and `kept_call`.
