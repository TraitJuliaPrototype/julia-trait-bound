# Design decisions

The decisions in force, grouped by the part of the design each one sets. It is in the agents' tier ([the record's index](INDEX.md)).

- **A decision that is not in a reference is not in force.** The reference states the rule; a row here says who decided it, when, and in which words.
- **Each row** gives what was decided, its status and origin (the date; the user's words verbatim where the user decided; "mine" for a choice an agent made while building), where it is stated, and whether it is built.
- **A row is unchanged since it was written.** It cites the sections and anchors of its day, and may name a mechanism by the term of its day. The references say how the rule reads now.
- **A decision that was replaced, withdrawn or whose mechanism is gone** is in [the ledger](../../attic/writeup/record/decisions-superseded.md), with the reason. The designs behind those rows are in [the alternatives](../design/alternatives.md), by topic.
- **A new decision:** first the reference, then a row here in the group it sets, in one commit.

**Statuses.**
- **adopted:** part of the design;
- **rejected:** considered and not taken; the rejection holds;
- **deferred:** designed or agreed, and not worked on;
- **pending:** waiting for a decision by the user.

**Abbreviations in the rows.** "s23" is session 23 of the session notes. "rev 988" is a revision of the design note of October 2026. D-n is a row of [the plan for the next iteration](../plans/2026-11-15-next-iteration.md). W-n is a work item ([the work queue](todo.md); closed ones in its ledger).
