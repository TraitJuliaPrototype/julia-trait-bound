# The registry of experiments

This is the registry of the project's experiments: proposed, running, done, superseded and abandoned. It is for agents and for the user: what was asked, what was run, where the scripts and logs are, and what came out.

Written 2026-10-20 from the experiments' READMEs, [the evidence](../design/evidence.md), [the state](../state.md), the reviews, the plans, the open issues and the explorations. The measurements themselves stay where they are; this page points to them.

## How to use it

- **Each experiment has an ID, `E-n`.** IDs are stable and never reused. They were assigned in document order on 2026-10-20; a later experiment takes the next free number, whatever its area.
- **A new experiment gets a row before it is run,** with status proposed or running and the question it answers. Name the decision criterion if there is one.
- **When it is done,** fill in the outcome with the key number(s), the date or build, and where it is written up.
- **When a later experiment or a design change replaces it,** set the status to superseded and name what replaced it. Do not delete the row.
- **An outcome says what a document states.** Where no document states one, the row says "not documented".

**Statuses.**
- **proposed:** written down, not started.
- **running:** started, no outcome yet.
- **done:** run, outcome recorded.
- **superseded:** replaced by a later experiment or by a design change.
- **abandoned:** dropped without an outcome.

**Builds and sessions.**
- **The round:** the measurement round of 2026-10-20, julia-1.13 3b8085c222, TraitWorld bed3782. Logs in `experiments/factscompiler/round/logs/`.
- **The build of 2026-10-19:** julia-1.13 61852d8fb1, TraitWorld 375a930.
- **The package prototype:** TraitWorld's own facts policy as a package, sessions 23–28, before the facts mode moved into Julia's compiler.
- **Sessions to dates:** s20–21 2026-10-15; s22–23 2026-10-16; s24–27 2026-10-17; s28 2026-10-17/18 (no session note); s29 2026-10-18 to 2026-10-20.
- **"Plain"** is the same code without traits; on the patched build it is patched-plain, not stock.

**Paths.** `experiments/` is the experiments repository at the repository root (from here: `../../experiments/`). The demonstrators are sibling repositories (`../../TraitDifferentialEquations/`, `../../TraitMakie/`, `../../TraitReport/`, `../../forks/`).
