# Open issues

Everything known to be open: the design, the prototype, the runtime and measurements, and the documents. The current design is the [references](reference/README.md), with the rationale in [`design.md`](design.md); the decisions, with their origins, are in [`record/decisions.md`](record/decisions.md). The current measurements are `design/evidence.md`; what Julia gained is `design/julia-changes.md`.

Only open items are listed in sections 1–6. Items keep their numbers, so that references such as "§1.10" stay valid; the gaps are closed items, which are listed at the end, under [Closed items](#closed-items), with how and where they were closed. Commits are in the repository named (julia-1.13, TraitWorld, a demonstrator).

**How items are kept** (the user's rule of 2026-10-27, [decisions](record/decisions.md) §8; applied by the clean-up, W-65, [its proposal](../attic/writeup/plans/2026-10-27-cleanup-issues-and-queue.md)): a principled choice, or one that simplifies at little cost, is adopted; one that working with trait code would inform waits for the ports (§6); one that evidence can settle names its experiment; otherwise it is accepted as it is. Only choices with a well-articulated trade-off wait for the user (§1). Performance work is in the [work queue](record/todo.md), or, where the rule for performance work does not take it up, in [the performance explorations log](record/performance-explorations.md).

- *GT-5.* **A closed type's contract promises no result** ([compiler §7.1](reference/compiler.md#71-the-rule), [§7.5](reference/compiler.md#75-io); open issue 171, closed; mine, 2026-11-10). `IO`'s members are unchecked, so each operation's result is `Any`: correct for every stream, and Julia's own answer at a receiver typed `IO`.

157. **In code compiled by facts, a function barrier loses its callee's declared result** (found 2026-11-07 by W-104's first slice).
   - *The case:* TraitMakie's recipes keep their data in fields typed by a trait (`pos::Positions`), and call kernels compiled per type (`limits(@specialize(pos::Positions))::NTuple{4,Float64}`). So the operation's declared result fails the membership check, at the library's recipes and at a user's recipe (`Ribbon`) that calls them.
   - *The rules that block it:* a run-time site's result is `Any` under facts ([facts §5](reference/facts.md#5-run-time-sites)).    - *Also:* the membership check infers its top frame plainly but its callees under their own policy, so a member is checked by a mix of the two.
   - *Proposals:*
     - (a) A run-time site in facts code types its result as the declared result `RT` of every method it could select now, asserted at the call, as an operation's is (§7.9). A later method returning something else raises `TypeError` there.
     - (b) An idiom, no rule: a kernel whose result callers rely on becomes an operation of the trait, with a default.
     - (c) The check infers under the serving method's own policy, whatever (a) or (b) gives.

     *Measured for (c)* (2026-11-08, E-311's first build, `unified` 917e767d73, which inferred reflection's top frame under the method's policy): TraitMakie fails to load, `MappedColors` refused as a `ColorSpec` because `rgba` infers `Any` at a function barrier.
