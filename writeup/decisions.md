# Design decisions: the register

**The current state is [`design.md`](design.md)** (§7 lists the decisions in force). This file is the full register, with history: rows marked **history** describe designs or mechanisms that were superseded, withdrawn or retired, and are kept for the record.

Every design decision with its status, as of 2026-10-18 (session 29). The notes say what the design is; this file says what was decided, when, and where it is written down. Statuses:
- **adopted:** in the design notes;
- **rejected:** considered and not taken;
- **deferred:** designed or agreed, but not worked on now;
- **pending:** waiting for a decision by the user.

"Prototype" says whether TraitWorld runs it.
