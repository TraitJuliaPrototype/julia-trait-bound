# Design decisions

The decisions in force, by topic, as of 2026-10-19. Each row says what was decided, when and by whom (with the user's words where the user decided), where the current documents state it, and what the prototype runs. The design itself is [`design.md`](design.md) and [`design/implementation.md`](design/implementation.md); §7 there lists the decisions in force in short. Designs that were tried and dropped are explained by topic in [`design/alternatives.md`](design/alternatives.md); the rows they had in this register are kept, with their wording, in [Superseded, withdrawn and retired](#superseded-withdrawn-and-retired) at the end.

Statuses:
- **adopted:** part of the design;
- **rejected:** considered and not taken; the rejection holds;
- **deferred:** designed or agreed, but not worked on now;
- **pending:** waiting for a decision by the user.

"s23" is session 23; "rev 988" is a revision of the live design note of 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-16. "Mine" marks a decision made while building, without the user. "Prototype" says whether TraitWorld (or julia-1.13) runs it.
