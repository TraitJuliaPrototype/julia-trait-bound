## 2. Choices (mine, recorded in the decision log and open issues §1)

- **Where the deep class is computed and kept:** the G2 bit itself, decided when a CodeInstance is finished from its own record and the bits of the CodeInstances among its edges. Callees finish first, so no walk. An inference cycle is decided as one. A callee named by its MethodInstance alone (no CodeInstance) counts as outside the class: its backedge carries its invalidation. The one-level bit has no reader left, so it is not kept beside the deep one.
- **D-6 is not built, and taken out of the references** (after first writing it in, with its fact held as a method-table edge and its result asserted). E-416 (c): as the plan scoped it, to code compiled by facts, it moves 3 of the forks' 4,799 own image instances into the class (1,983 to 1,986), upstream's 1 (1,382 to 1,383); E-395's 61 %/63 % took the rule at every caller, plain Base code included. Also, a method-table edge is not reached by a redefinition at the same signature (Julia invalidates a replaced method through its instances' backedges), and an instance edge carries the callee's own invalidation: Julia has no edge form for "the fact, not the code". Open issue 194 (proposal (a), mine: Base's throwing helpers made deep G2 in stage 5), GT-21 restated as the tension G-1 against G-4. C-92 retired; E-418 not run.
- **Strict mode's walk by the deep class** is in the library (`src/strict.jl`): F-25. It needs a change of the library, in a worktree of its own (`TraitWorld-w155`), to be merged by the main session.
