## Finding 1: an error path of the machinery invalidated every entry

`using DataFrames` (and `using CSV`) invalidated the cached code of every
entry of Columns, Stats and Reports at once (checked with the CodeInstance
world ranges). The chain, from SnoopCompile's trees walked down to the
entries: `colmean(::Any, ::Int)` → `enter` → `_rule3_error(f, pos, t::TObj)`
→ `_traitlist` → `string.(uniontypes(T))` (a broadcast over `Vector{Any}`)
→ `string(::Integer)` → `convert(UInt64, Integer)`, which SentinelArrays
extends; via CSV, DataStrings' `convert(String, AbstractString)` through the
same broadcast. A `@noinline` call is still a static edge. Fix in
`entry.jl`: every error and reflection path reachable from an entry is
behind `Base.inferencebarrier` (`_rule3_error`, `_closed_miss`, `_kw_refused`,
`_has_tagged_fallback` in `fallback`/`kwfallback`, `_tw_type`, the new
`_callas_mismatch`). After the fix every entry stays valid through `using
DataFrames`, `using DataFramesInterface` and `using CSV`; the first
`zscores(df, 1)` of a session compiles nothing. This is finding 7 of
`poc/world/README.md` ("the machinery must obey the design") with a
sharper rule: **no static edge from an entry into message formatting or
reflection; `@noinline` is not enough.** Goes into the note and
`design-findings.md`.

## Finding 2: the audit's `Base._which` recompiles Base on the first call

After the fix, the first `summarize(df)` still compiled ~38 ms, of which
inference was a single frame: `Compiler.findsup_mt(::Any, ::UInt, ::Nothing)`,
11 ms, "# recompile": Base's own method lookup, invalidated by DataFrames'
dependencies, reached through `audit` → `Base._which` on the first call of
each signature per session. Prepared, not applied: replace `Base._which`
in `audit`, `_current_target` and `_has_tagged_fallback` by a direct
`ccall(:jl_gf_invoke_lookup_worlds, Any, (Any, Any, UInt, Ptr{Csize_t}, Ptr{Csize_t}), sig, nothing, world, min, max)`
returning a `Core.MethodMatch` (a `_match(sig, world)` helper in
`audit.jl`); `verify.jl`'s uses are build-time and can stay.
