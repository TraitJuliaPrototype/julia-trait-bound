## 4. C-74 (E-311)

- The fix (compiler §2.3): the image's queue infers a MethodInstance under its method's policy as the queue reaches it; the image's queue and the JIT's infer a CodeInstance again under the policy it records; the other policy's interpreter shares the codegen cache.
