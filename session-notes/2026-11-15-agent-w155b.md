## 4. Choices (mine)

- **GT-23 (c): the default is a trait module's, not every module's that declares `facts`** (E-431). A scope option `owner_only`, on in `@trait_module`, off for `@compiler_options facts = true` alone. Upstream compiled by facts (the path's first step) would otherwise make 86 methods pirates at Tables' interface, and the library's lowering 8 at its own internals; the forks and the ports keep the gain, since their code compiled by facts is in trait modules.
