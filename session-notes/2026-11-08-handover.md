## Pitfalls met today

- **Background jobs:** use the tool's background mode, not `&` (no notification otherwise).
- **`git add -A` in `experiments/`** once committed a 447 MB core dump from a crashed test; `.gitignore` now has `core-*`. Look at what a commit stages.
- **Do not edit TraitWorld** while a battery or a precompile loads it from source.
- **The third run** of a session is sometimes slow on this host: take the median of three.
- **Code compiled by facts** makes a call with an abstract argument a run-time site, compiled per runtime type: a cache keyed by a type or a closure that captures one is compiled again for each (E-308).
- **A workload's entry points** called inside its closure are inlined there and get no instance of their own: call them through `invokelatest` (E-306).
- **Plain code in images** is invalidated by plain packages' methods through untyped calls: a range over an untyped length reaches Base's promotion (E-307); an untyped `codeunits` (E-302). `colon_scan.jl` finds the ranges.
- **Records:** register the experiment's row before running; a finished experiment's outcome, evidence and `state.md` in one commit; logs under `experiments/`, never only in the scratchpad.
