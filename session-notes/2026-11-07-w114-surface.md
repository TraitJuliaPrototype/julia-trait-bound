## Fully typed mode (`unified` 5f2373b2d5; TraitWorld 7adcc94)

- **Where it lives:** the compiler finds the fault, the library explains it. A method's meta or its module's option puts it under the facts policy; at the end of inference, a run-time site (in it or a facts callee: the `nortsite` effect) or an abstract result that is no set makes the optimizer put `jl_not_fully_typed(svec(...))` at the code's start, and the code's effects stop being foldable. The call reaches the library's hook, which builds `fully_typed`'s report (a walk over the code inferred by facts, with root causes through fields, elements, results) and raises `NotFullyTypedError` or `AbstractTypeError`, or warns once. Fully typed code carries nothing. A prologue the macro adds was the alternative; it cannot reach a module's later methods.
- The new symbols needed their `jl_symbol` in `ast.c` too; without it the meta was silently ignored (the first build).
- Concreteness per trait (in the contract, checked at each membership) and per method (a check per named part, a constant where the call is compiled).
- `AC_16` re-judged to the reference: the call raises when it runs, not `code_typed` when it compiles. 21 of 21 acceptance cases in both settings.

- **Three weak points found by Base's own types:**
  1. `Keyed`'s `haskey(d, k::K)` tied with Base's `haskey(d::AbstractDict, k)`: the trait's method typed a further argument by a parameter the receiver fixes. Lowering now types one only where it binds an input.
  2. `mul!(y, A::LinearOperator, x)` hid LinearAlgebra's untyped fallback for every matrix (a set beats `Any`), and invalidated its callers at each load (E-281). `LinearOp`'s operation is BaseTraits' `apply!`; a member whose serving method takes any receiver is refused at its check; open issue 150.
  3. A `KeySet` and a `BitMatrix` read as ambiguous: method matching read interferences recorded when the method at the set was inserted, before the membership made the two methods meet. A pair with a method at a set is now asked in the query's world (`unified` 0ce5b7f752).
- E-282: Base's `arrayops` and `dict` pass with BaseTraits loaded, at their times without it.
