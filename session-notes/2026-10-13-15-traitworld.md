## Findings (see the README)

- Rule 5 must apply to an implementation's methods.
- A type's own operations never fall back to the representation's.
- Inference confirms conformance only at concrete argument types.
- Check results are kept per signature in compiled code.
- The machinery obeys the design.
- Entries are analyzed at their call sites.

## Review

A Fable review (the user's request) found ten bugs:
- the rule-3 check stopped at abstract argument types;
- a trait object could escape by return value;
- a fallback of another shape was missed;
- a `where`-clause method could replace an entry;
- defaults could not use supertraits;
- existing methods ignored defaults;
- the projection-with-operations form was rejected;
- lower bounds conferred ownership;
- another module's unrelated bounds were not checked;
- caches were unlocked.

It also found:
- an over-strict ambiguity check between entries;
- trait-world membership was judged from the outer type;
- every unrelated method definition flushed every entry cache;
- quadratic ownership checks;
- overclaims in the README.

All fixed and tested (117 tests). The table is in `poc/world/README.md`.
