- **Designed here** (the plan had only the synthesis' §3.4 (c) and open issue 180 (c)): every rule is a method's deletion's.

  - What ends: callers whose selection at the member changes; instances of methods at the set compiled at the member, with their callers; code that folded the positive (through its edge to the membership's method). What stays: calls that dispatched to the member's own method, splits whose branch is the member's own method.
  - Who may: who may declare it; a pirate's otherwise; strict mode as a method's deletion (facts §11.3's row).
  - Not alone: a member by its definition, a supertrait's membership, `Dynamic{X}`'s.
  - Values stored before stay; calls on them dispatch by type (G1 holds).
  - Images hold memberships in force; a later image whose code relied on a taken-back membership fails validation on the method's edge.
  - Revise: the library's extension for Revise lists the memberships an expression declared and takes back those a revised file no longer has.
