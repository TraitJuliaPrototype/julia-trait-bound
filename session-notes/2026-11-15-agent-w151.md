## 1. What is built

**The library** (TraitWorld `unified` 5e2ef71):
- `Compiled{T}` (`src/compiled.jl`): the wrapper. `compiled_for(x)` is `TraitWorld.ByType.compiled_for`, an ordinary function, which a module binds explicitly (`using TraitWorld.ByType: compiled_for`), since the library still exports the cast of that name for the ports not yet converted. Its operations forward through `_fwd` (as a box's do); its fields, `getproperty`, `setproperty!` and calls forward to the object; `unwrap`; `Compiled(d)` of a box is the wrapper of its object; `Dynamic{X}(w)` boxes the object.
- `@trait` declares `Compiled{Z} where {P…, Z <: X{P…}}` in `X{P…}`, forwarding by `object`; `Callable` likewise (`src/callable.jl`). The member hook skips the family as it skips `Dynamic`'s.
- `@default_dynamic` is recorded on the contract and no longer sets the compiler's default bit (step 4). `erases(X)` reads it, and `Callable`'s bit from Julia.
- `@erasing` (`src/erasing.jl`) at a method definition: for each argument typed by a trait that erases (a trait, an intersection with such a component, a `where` variable bounded by one), the method is defined at `Union{Dynamic{<:X}, Compiled{<:X}}`, and one forwarder per positional arity that holds such an argument: `f(@nospecialize(t::X), …; kws...) = f(Dynamic{X}(t), …; kws...)`. With several such arguments the one forwarder boxes the plain ones (`_box_plain`). A parametric trait written bare or with `<:` parameters cannot be boxed there: the method is left as written. The method at the kept forms is defined before the forwarders.
- `convert(::Type{Dynamic{X}}, x)`, and the identity at a box of `X`.
- D-3 (`src/atbox.jl`): `check_at_box(m)` reads the method's instance at `box_signature(m)` (each argument at a trait, a kept form or a `where` variable bounded by a trait, replaced by `Dynamic{X}`; free variables `Float64` where allowed), inferred by the facts rule and optimized. A fault: a call with a value at the box that finds no method (directly; below a call that only throws, the chain followed and named; left to run time with no method at the known types), or that only throws (an undeclared field). Reported at the outermost frame's line, the line of the method that passes the value. `check_definitions` and the `bounded` refusal read it; the keyword sorter is skipped (its body method is read); forwarders are skipped. A method whose body names a global not yet defined, or whose only faults are calls of the module's own functions with no method yet, waits for those names and at last for the module's end.
- Tests: `test/bytype.jl` (new); the attribute's, the classification's, the messages' and fully typed mode's tests follow the new rules. The suite passes.

**The ODE port** (TDE `unified` 813808a): `ODEProblem` with `@erasing` (no `keep`, no `Erased`); the problem's `F` is `Dynamic{…}` or `Compiled{PartialFn{…}}`; a record's functions each in their own form (open issue 159); `FunctionCallback` and `FunctionEventCallback` parametrized by their functions' forms, built by `@erasing` constructors (three and four trait arguments); TraitIndexing's methods at `IndexProvider` with `@erasing`; every trait module `first_seen = false`; the tests bind `compiled_for` from `ByType`. The suite passes, 35 testsets.

**NetworkDynamics** (in progress): the module `first_seen = false`; `compiled_for` from `ByType`; `_kept_model` with `@erasing` returns its argument (the box); the user's value boxed where its record is made; `_run_batch!`, `run_*_batch!`, `_vertex!`, `_edge!`, `_user_*`, `_static_facts` with `@erasing`; the kernel's call `compiled_for(batch.model)` unchanged in spelling, now the wrapper of the box's object.

## 2. Choices made (mine)

- **The wrapper's name: `Compiled{T}`,** beside `Dynamic{X}`: the pair names what code is compiled for. `T` is the object's type, not a trait.
- **The forwarder's form: a macro at the method definition, `@erasing`.** It stands for lowering: Julia's lowering already makes several methods of one definition (a positional default, a keyword sorter). It decides by the traits' declarations when the definition is expanded; the runtime's method definition could decide by the evaluated signature with nothing written. `@default_dynamic` hooking definitions was not taken: the definition hook cannot narrow a method, and redefining it is not allowed while an image is built.
- **The kept forms as a `Union` of the two families,** not a type of their own: the argument is then no trait argument for the compiler, so the attribute's machinery stays out of the way (no step 4, no fallback by scope), and dispatch ranks it above the forwarder at `X`.
- **The trait's own operation methods (defaults included) are not forwarded.** A plain member served by a default runs it as itself; the wrapper reaches it through `_fwd`.
- **An intersection erases where any component erases.**
- **A trait whose parameters the signature leaves open is not boxed there** (the box needs the parameters, traits §7.7).
- **No exception for Julia's own types** (step 4's): every plain member is boxed by the forwarder, `abs2` at a `Callable` argument included.
- **The wrapper's call method takes any arguments,** so a wrapper is a member of every `Callable` by Julia's rule, as every function will be under D-2. A wrapper of a function with no method at the signature reaches the forwarder, whose box refuses it where the value is passed (`TypeError`).
- **D-3's faults are calls that find no method or only throw.** A call left to run time that finds a method when it runs is Julia's barrier, no fault: E-397's `setproperty!` with a name that is no constant passes now. `typeof(t)` and `Wrapped(t)` are bounded.
- **The ports' trait modules declare `first_seen = false`**: D-1 has no fallback by scope.
